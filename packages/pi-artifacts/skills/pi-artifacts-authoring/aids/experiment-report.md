# Experiment report

**Reader goal:** Help someone decide whether a measured change is worthwhile.

Every section is optional. A small output is valid: describe the change, the comparison, and the
conclusion without filling every section.

## Optional: Question and expectation

State what the experiment is trying to learn and what result would change the decision.

## Optional: Setup and versions

Name the baseline, candidate, relevant configuration, revision, and repeat plan.

## Optional: Results

Summarize the comparison and useful measures such as quality, cost, time, or error rate. Link to
raw data when the reader needs to inspect it.

## Optional: Limitations

Explain missing cases, noisy conditions, or reasons not to generalize the result.

## Optional: Conclusion and next step

Say what the result supports and what should happen next.
