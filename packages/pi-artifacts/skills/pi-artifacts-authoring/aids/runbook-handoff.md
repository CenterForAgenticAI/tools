# Runbook/handoff

**Reader goal:** Help someone safely execute or resume work.

Every section is optional. A small output is valid: provide only the ordered action, dependency, and
check needed for the next person.

## Optional: Purpose and starting point

Name the outcome, current state, and assumptions a reader needs before beginning.

## Optional: Ordered steps

List the actions in order. Include commands, decision points, and dependencies only where they help
the next person proceed.

## Optional: Changed or open items

For a handoff, summarize what changed, what remains open, and what needs the recipient's attention.

## Optional: Safety and recovery

Describe a rollback, stop condition, or recovery path when one is needed.

## Optional: Verification

State how to tell that the work is complete, including the relevant check or observable result.

## Optional: References

Link to files, reports, revisions, or people that make the handoff actionable.
