# Recommendation/design memo

**Reader goal:** Help someone choose a direction and understand the trade-offs.

Every section is optional. A small output is valid: state the recommendation and the one or two
facts needed to act.

## Optional: Recommendation

State the proposed choice in a sentence and say who should make it.

## Optional: Why this choice

Give the few observations or constraints that make the choice useful. Link to supporting artifacts
when needed instead of reproducing every detail.

## Optional: Alternatives and trade-offs

Name plausible alternatives and the cost, benefit, or risk of each.

## Optional: Concrete next move

List a small change, experiment, or decision that follows from the recommendation.

## Optional: Unresolved decisions

Call out questions that still need an owner or a choice.
