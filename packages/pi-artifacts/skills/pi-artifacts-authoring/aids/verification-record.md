# Verification record

**Reader goal:** Let a reader check what was demonstrated and how to repeat the check.

Every section is optional. A small output is valid: record one scenario, its revision and
environment, and the result.

## Optional: Scenario and expected result

Name the behavior being checked and what a successful result looks like.

## Optional: Revision and environment

Record the exact revision, relevant tools or configuration, and whether the result came from a live
system or a fixture.

## Optional: Observations

Summarize the result, with links to screenshots, traces, logs, or data when they are useful.

## Optional: Gaps

Name cases not checked, unavailable inputs, or follow-up checks.

## Optional: File manifest

List the generated files or bundles a reader should keep with this record.
