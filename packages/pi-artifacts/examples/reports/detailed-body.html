Generator contract

The helper accepts a report kind, narrative and evidence body fragments, an output path, and metadata. It rejects missing or duplicate section identifiers, unknown evidence mappings, body scripts, and non-inline resource references.

node scripts/make-report.mjs \
  --kind combined \
  --body examples/reports/narrative-body.html \
  --evidence examples/reports/detailed-body.html \
  --meta examples/reports/combined-meta.json \
  --out examples/reports/demo-combined.html
